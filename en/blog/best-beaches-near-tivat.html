The best beaches near Tivat: a guide for apartment guests

15 October 2026

Tivat has no single large town beach, but the surroundings offer several excellent places to swim. Here are the four our guests choose most often, with the basics to help you decide where to spend the day.

Kalardovo – a sandy beach for families

Kalardovo is about 2 km from our apartments, close to the airport and the Island of Flowers. The beach is sandy and about 250 m long, and the shallow water and the usual beach furniture make it ideal for families with small children. There is no accommodation nearby, so it is a typical day-trip beach: no bus goes there, so you will need a car or a taxi. Parking is available in front of the beach.

Župa beach – shade under pine trees

A small sandy beach about 70 m long, in a bay just before the entrance to Tivat from the south. Next to the beach is a dense pine forest that gives natural shade even on the hottest days, which makes it a good choice for a quieter day by the sea.

Plavi Horizonti – the largest beach in the Tivat area

It lies at the bottom of Pržno bay and is about 350 m long, covered with fine sand said to have healing properties, especially for rheumatic conditions. The sand stretches far into the shallow sea, and next to the beach there are a pine forest and olive groves. The beach is fully equipped with showers and toilets, and there are restaurants and cafés, sports courts and parking nearby.

Jaz – a day trip to Budva

Jaz is one of the most popular beaches in Montenegro, about 3 km west of Budva. It is about 800 m long and split into two parts: the smaller one is pebbly, the larger one sandy. It is surrounded by bars and cafés, and in summer it hosts concerts and events. Plan a full day for this trip.

Quick beach tips

  • Bring drinking water, high-factor sunscreen and water shoes.
  • Mornings and late afternoons are less crowded and more pleasant.
  • For Kalardovo and Jaz, plan your transport: a car or a taxi.
  • Shade is easiest to find at Župa, so it is a good choice for midday.
