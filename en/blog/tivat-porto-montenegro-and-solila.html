Tivat at a slow pace: Porto Montenegro, the promenade and Solila

17 October 2026

Tivat is the calmest town in the Bay of Kotor, ideal for a slow-paced holiday. Here is a suggestion for an easy day, mostly on foot.

The seaside promenade and the town park

The seaside promenade leads past beaches, cafés and restaurants. There are also shady parks, so the walk is pleasant even in the warmer hours.

Porto Montenegro

The Porto Montenegro marina, with its yachts and elegant waterfront, attracts guests from all over the world. It is enough to stroll along the waterfront, have a coffee and look at the yachts. It is about 1 km from our apartments.

Solila – a bird habitat

For nature lovers there is Solila, a protected bird habitat (an ornithological reserve). Bring binoculars and a camera; early morning and early evening are best for birdwatching.

The Island of Flowers

The Island of Flowers, just off the coast, is a nice goal for a shorter outing and part of the story of Tivat as a green, calm town.

A plan for one day

  • Morning: coffee and a walk by the sea.
  • Midday: shade and lunch in town.
  • Afternoon: Porto Montenegro and a stroll along the waterfront.
  • Evening: dinner by the sea.
