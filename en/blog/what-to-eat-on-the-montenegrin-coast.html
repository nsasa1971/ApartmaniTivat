What to eat on the Montenegrin coast: five dishes you should not miss

18 October 2026

The Montenegrin coast is known for simple Mediterranean cuisine based on fresh fish, olive oil and homemade specialities. Here are five dishes worth trying.

Grilled fish

Fresh fish grilled over charcoal, served with olive oil and lemon. In most restaurants fish is priced by the kilogram, so ask for the price before you order.

Black risotto

Cuttlefish risotto, dark from the cuttlefish ink – a common choice on the coast. You will recognise it by its colour, and the flavour is rich and of the sea.

Brudet

A traditional dish of fish stewed in a sauce of tomatoes and olive oil. It is often served with polenta.

Cured ham and cheese

Homemade cured ham and cheese are a classic cold appetiser with a glass of wine. Try them with a Montenegrin wine, for example Vranac.

Olive oil and olives

Olives and olive oil are the foundation of the Mediterranean table, and olive groves are part of the landscape of Tivat – and of the yard of our apartments.

The apartments have a fully equipped kitchen and an outdoor grill, so you can also cook fish and vegetables from the nearby markets yourself.
